================================================================================
 QUANTUM FORGE - TOP-BAR ICON REFERENCES
 Seven utility keys, two verb badges. Drawn as vectors in Icons.luau.
 Same contract as 16-salvage-art-prompts.txt: intent, prompt, negative,
 settings, save path.
================================================================================

READ THIS FIRST
These keys are NOT uploaded images. Icons.luau draws them at runtime from Frames
and labels, so a 22px mark scales cleanly, tint into any accent, and costs no
asset request. The prompts below produce a REFERENCE SHEET used to redraw the
vector, not a file that ships. Do not add these PNGs to AssetManifest.

WHAT THE SHEET HAS TO FIX
- the silhouette at 22px, which is the size the top bar actually uses
- the amount of interior detail that survives at 22px (almost none)
- the stroke weight relative to the 22px box: one 3px stem, never two thin ones
- where the accent colour sits, if anywhere

THE HOUSE RULES
- Solid single-colour marks on a flat light field, drawn as if inked with a
  2.2mm pen: slightly uneven, one line weight, rounded ends.
- No gradients, no bevels, no shadows, no glow, no two-tone interior.
- 45-degree and 90-degree strokes only. No curves tighter than the pen can cut.
- The sheet is a drawing to copy, so show construction lines and a 3x enlargement
  beside the 22px version. The small one is the test.
- No text, no letters, no numbers in the image.

WHERE THEY GO
Reference only. Redraw in src/client/Icons.luau as a function returning a Frame of
bars. When the vector is done, delete the sheet from the repo (keep it in Drive if
you want a record). The manifest must not gain an id for these.

HOW THE KEYS ARE USED
50 x 62 key, 22px icon at the top (roughly y 10 to 32), 13px label under it.
Seven keys in a row: VAULT, CODEX, BOARDS, TRADE, NOTES, GUIDE, SETUP. Then ASCEND
(88 wide) and DAILY (78 wide) with a small vector badge, not an icon.

COMMON NEGATIVE (every prompt)
text, letters, numbers, words, logo, watermark, signature, gradient, bevel, drop
shadow, glow, bloom, two-tone, outline only, sketchy hatching, perspective,
isometric, 3D render, photograph, colour background, multiple icons per prompt
unless the sheet asks for a row.


================================================================================
 1. vault  -  the blade vault
================================================================================

Intent: a strongbox you would keep a weapon in. Not a padlock: this bank is a chest
with a keyhole, and the keyhole is what makes it read at 22px.

SILHOUETTE AT 22PX
A squat rectangle, wider than tall, with a shallow arched lid and one round hole
low centre. The hole must be at least 4px at 22px or it disappears.

PROMPT
Hand-inked one-colour icon reference sheet on a flat bone field (#E8E0D0). Nine
constructions of the same mark: one large 132px, one 66px, one 22px, and six small
variations of the lid arch. A squat strongbox with a shallow arched lid, a full
width band across the top third and a keyhole cut clean through the lower centre.
The lid band is a solid bar, the body is a solid block, the keyhole is the only
hole. Pen line is one weight, slightly uneven, rounded ends. No interior hatching.

PALETTE: ink only, deep navy-black (#0F1117) on bone. One version may show the
accent placement as a single flat cyan (#5AD1E6) bar across the lid band.

SETTINGS: 1024 x 1024. High contrast, flat fill, no gradients.

SAVE: assets/icons/reference/vault.png

 4. trade  -  the trading desk
================================================================================

Intent: two arrows swapping. In one colour at 22px the pair reads as exchange, and
it is the only mark with a diagonal, so it stands out in the row.

SILHOUETTE AT 22PX
Two parallel arrows, one up-right, one down-left, offset so the heads do not
collide. Heads are solid triangles, shafts are 3px at 22px.

PROMPT
Hand-inked one-colour icon reference sheet on a flat bone field (#E8E0D0). Nine
constructions of the same mark: one large 132px, one 66px, one 22px, and six small
variations of arrow offset. Two exchange arrows: the upper one points up-right, the
lower one points down-left, both with solid triangular heads and straight shafts,
offset so the heads clear each other. Pen line is one weight, rounded shafts. No
curves, no circle around them.

PALETTE: ink only, deep navy-black (#0F1117) on bone. One version may show one
arrow in flat cyan (#5AD1E6) and the other in ink.

SETTINGS: 1024 x 1024. High contrast, flat.

SAVE: assets/icons/reference/trade.png

 CHECKLIST BEFORE REDRAWING
================================================================================
[ ] At 22px the mark is still recognisable as itself and not as its neighbour.
[ ] One line weight, 3px in the 22px box; no 1px detail anywhere.
[ ] The mark is a solid fill, not an outline of a shape.
[ ] Nothing depends on colour to be legible.
[ ] The seven marks are distinguishable in a row at arm's length from the screen.
[ ] The codex and guide marks are different silhouettes, not the same book twice.
[ ] No PNG is added to AssetManifest for any of these.
