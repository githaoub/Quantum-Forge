================================================================================
 QUANTUM FORGE - SALVAGE ART PROMPTS
 4 cache rarities + 4 codex set emblems. Same contract as 15-stuff.txt:
 intent, prompt, negative, settings, save path.
================================================================================

WHY THIS SET EXISTS
The Salvage rack ships without art on purpose: the slots are typographic and the
rarity colour does the work, so the loop is playable with zero uploads. These
eight images are the upgrade, not the dependency.

WHERE THEY GO
Save the JPGs to assets/badges/source/, run each through
  python scripts/prepare_gamepass_art.py --src assets/badges/source/<slug>.jpg
    --dst assets/badges/<slug>.png --no-preview
then add the ids to AssetManifest.UiArt under the cache_ / set_ keys.

THE HOUSE RULES (same as the fifteen badges)
- Painted by a person: gouache, ink, coloured pencil. Tabletop game box register.
- Bright field, ink subject. The disc lives on a dark panel at 40-88px.
- Colour-first palette. Shadows are coloured umber or indigo, never grey or black.
- The ink contour is the only true black in the image.
- Human tells are requirements: paint over the contour once, dry-brush, pigment
  specks, slight asymmetry. Perfectly clean edges are a machine tell.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, ever.

 1. cache_common  -  "Scrap Cache"
================================================================================

Intent: the bottom of the loot table, and it should still be a pleasure to open.
A dented tin crate, wire-bound, lid ajar, one nail already escaped. The picture
says "somebody already threw this away once".

The difference from Alloy matters: this one is salvage, not manufacture. Dents,
mis-matched panels, a bent hinge. Alloy is a clean cast canister.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PROMPT - paste into the prompt field
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hand-painted game badge illustration, perfectly circular, centred on a square
canvas with fully transparent corners. Painted by a professional illustrator in
gouache, ink and coloured pencil, the kind of original art printed on a tabletop
game box or a mid-century children's book plate. Rich, saturated, hand-mixed
colour, layered wet into wet and then drawn over while damp. Not a vector icon,
not clipart, not a 3D render, not an app icon.

SUBJECT: a squat salvage crate made from three different sheets of mis-matched
tin, hammered flat and bound with two loops of twisted wire. The lid is ajar by a
finger's width and one long nail has escaped to hang on the rim. A bent iron
handle sits on top, slightly off centre because the hinge was replaced. Two dents
on the front panel, one with a crease. A single rivet has popped and left a hole.
Nothing else in the frame: no background, no ground, no second object.

PALETTE: a low-contrast warm-neutral harmony, the quietest badge in the set. Field
bone (#E8E0D0) with ash grey (#B9B3A6) at the rim and a pale cream (#FFF6E4) sweep
upper left. The tin is slate blue-grey (#7E8C99) over warm grey (#A9A296), its
wire binding rust (#A85C33), the escaped nail brass (#D8A44B). Shadows are umber
(#5E4632), never grey. The ink contour is near-black (#141018) and it is the only
true black in the image.

LINE: confident hand-inked contours, a loaded brush line whose weight swells and
tapers, with the small live wobble of a real hand. The outer disc edge gets the
same treatment. Contours are the strongest value on the page and never break.

LIGHT AND SHADOW: one warm key from the upper left, one cool bounce from below.
Shading is painted as flat shapes with crisp edges, two tones per material, never
airbrushed. Warm light means cool shadows: the slate tin goes to indigo-grey in
shadow, not to black.

SURFACE: fine tooth of cold-press watercolour paper, gentle granulation in the
ash rim, a scatter of pigment specks, one dry-brush scratch along the dented
panel. The paint sits slightly over the contour in one or two places, the way a
hand works. Perfectly clean edges are a machine tell. The texture is a whisper,
never a layer of noise.

LEGIBILITY: this prints at 40 pixels on the rack. One crate, one ajar lid, one
escaped nail. Minimum feature size 5% of the canvas. The dents must not read as
damage noise at thumbnail size; keep them broad and few.

COMPOSITION: 1:1 square, 1024x1024, badge centred, disc radius about 47% of the
canvas leaving a clean empty margin at the edge. Slight asymmetry: the crate sits
a touch left of centre and the lid tilts right.
