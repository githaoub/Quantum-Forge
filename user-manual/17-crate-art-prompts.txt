================================================================================
 QUANTUM FORGE - CRATE MODEL SHEETS + THE BOOST BADGE
 Five crate families for CratePreview.luau, plus the x2 badge redraw.
 Same contract as 16-salvage-art-prompts.txt: intent, prompt, negative,
 settings, save path.
================================================================================

WHY THIS SET EXISTS
The five crates are built in-engine from parts (CratePreview.luau), so the game
never needs these files to run. What it needs is a reference sheet that fixes the
silhouette, the part count and the palette before the builder is tuned. One sheet
per crate means the procedural model and a later mesh upload are the same object,
not two guesses.

WHAT TO DELIVER
Each prompt asks for a MODEL SHEET, not a beauty render:
  - one three-quarter view, large, centre
  - one front elevation, small, lower left, same height
  - one top-down silhouette, small, lower right
  - a flat mid-dark background so the shape reads
  - no text, no numbers, no watermark, no logo
The sheet is a drawing to build from, so exposed seams, hinge counts and panel
breaks matter more than lighting.

THE HOUSE RULES (same as every other asset in this project)
- Painted by a person: gouache, ink and coloured pencil. Tabletop game box
  register, the same hand as the salvage badges.
- The ink contour is the only true black. Every shadow is coloured umber, indigo
  or deep green.
- The crate sits on a dark panel in game at 192-268px, so the silhouette has to
  read at 96px and the palette has to survive a dark surround.
- Human tells are requirements: one pass of paint over the contour, dry-brush
  edges, pigment specks, a slightly off hinge. Clean vector edges are a machine
  tell and are wrong for this set.
- No text, no letters, no numbers, ever. No Roblox logo, no QR, no signature.

WHERE THEY GO
Reference sheets only. Save to assets/crates/reference/<slug>.png and keep them
out of the build; if a crate is ever promoted to a mesh, run the render through
  python scripts/prepare_gamepass_art.py --src <file> --dst assets/crates/<slug>.png
and add the id under AssetManifest.CrateArt.<slug>. Until then the procedural
builder is the shipping path.


================================================================================
 1. crate_standard  -  "Standard Forge Crate"
================================================================================

Intent: the yard crate. Bolted, banded, and honest about the odds. This is the
first object a new player opens, so it has to look like it was built by someone
who had a nail gun and a deadline.

MODEL NOTES
- A squat box, slightly wider than tall, 4 x 3.2 x 4 studs.
- Corrugated steel sides with a visible vertical seam; three horizontal bands.
- Lid hinged at the back with two crude hinges, lid sits 2cm proud.
- One latch, centred, oversized, the only brass on the object.
- A stencilled chevron on the front that is worn, not printed. It must be a
  painted shape, never a letterform.

PROMPT
Hand-painted model sheet for a game object, drawn by a professional illustrator
in gouache, ink and coloured pencil on a flat charcoal field (#23262E), the kind
of original concept plate printed in a tabletop game art book. Three-quarter view
large in the centre, a small front elevation lower left and a small top-down
silhouette lower right, all the same design at the same scale, evenly spaced on
one sheet. Not a vector icon, not clipart, not a 3D render, not an app icon.

SUBJECT: a squat industrial loot crate made of corrugated steel, wider than it is
tall. Three horizontal steel bands wrap the body, each with two visible rivets.
Two stubby hinges at the back hold a lid that sits slightly proud, and a single
oversized brass latch sits dead centre at the front. The lid carries a worn
hand-painted chevron, chipped at the edges. One corner is dented and the paint has
flaked there to show bare steel. No background detail, no ground shadow, no second
object.

PALETTE: warm ochre field on the crate, steel blue-grey body (#5C6B7A to #8FA0B0),
oxidised brass latch (#C8923A), rust creeping from two rivets (#A85C33). The
charcoal sheet field (#23262E) is flat and even. Ink contour over everything, then
one pass of paint across it so the line breaks at the corners.

NEGATIVE
photograph, 3D render, octane, glossy plastic, vector, flat design, minimal,
gradient mesh, neon, glow, bloom, text, letters, numbers, logo, watermark,
signature, drop shadow, floor, background scenery, extra crates, hands, person.

SETTINGS
1024 x 1024, square. High detail, medium contrast. No transparency needed: the
sheet is a plate. If the tool supports a reference, feed the standard crate render
from the live game for palette only.

SAVE
assets/crates/reference/crate_standard.png

 2. crate_void  -  "Void Capsule"
================================================================================

Intent: sealed in the dark. Something inside is still counting. This is the first
crate that costs real money to open, so it must look like it was made by someone
who does not want it opened.

MODEL NOTES
- A vertical capsule, 2.2 x 4 x 2.2 studs, rounded shoulders, flat base.
- Two halves split on the long axis with a hairline seam and a single keyed band.
- The band carries three small indicator lights, all the same size, none lit.
- A recessed circular port on the front, the only circular shape on the object.
- The base has four claws that bite into the floor, not legs.
- Void is a colour, not a fog effect: deep indigo and black with one cold edge.

PROMPT
Hand-painted model sheet for a game object, drawn by a professional illustrator
in gouache, ink and coloured pencil on a flat charcoal field (#23262E), the kind
of original concept plate printed in a tabletop game art book. Three-quarter view
large in the centre, a small front elevation lower left and a small top-down
silhouette lower right, all the same design at the same scale. Not a vector icon,
not clipart, not a 3D render.

SUBJECT: a sealed vertical capsule, rounded at the shoulders, flat at the base,
split into two halves by a hairline seam that runs the full height. A wide keyed
band wraps the middle, holding three identical unlit glass indicators no larger
than a thumbnail. A recessed circular port sits on the front, slightly below the
band, with no handle and no obvious way to turn it. Four short claws at the base
bite downward. The surface is matte and cold, with one polished edge catching
light at the seam. No fog, no particles, no light beams, no second object.

PALETTE: deep indigo body (#241E3B to #3B2E5C), a violet edge at the seam
(#7B4BC4), black-blue shadow (#14111F), one cold highlight in pale lilac
(#C9B8E8). The three indicators are unlit smoked glass (#4A4258). The charcoal
sheet field stays flat around the object.

NEGATIVE
fog, smoke, particles, glow, bloom, neon tube, sci-fi interface, hologram, text,
letters, numbers, logo, watermark, photograph, 3D render, glossy plastic, vector,
clipart, floor, background scenery, extra objects, person.

SETTINGS
1024 x 1024, square. High detail, low-key lighting described in paint, not bloom.

 3. crate_quantum  -  "Quantum Relic"
================================================================================

Intent: two states at once, both of them sharp. The joke is that the crate is an
obelisk that cannot decide whether it is open or closed, and the drawing should
show both readings at once without any effects.

MODEL NOTES
- An obelisk, 1.8 x 4.4 x 1.8 studs, four faces, slight taper to a blunt top.
- A single vertical channel cut down the front, offset one face to the left.
- The channel holds a floating blade-like sliver that never touches the walls.
- The top face is cut at an angle, as if a corner were sliced off and set aside.
- Shards orbit the base, frozen mid-fall, none touching the ground.
- Quantum is the pink-violet band already used in the HUD, not a new colour.

PROMPT
Hand-painted model sheet for a game object, drawn by a professional illustrator
in gouache, ink and coloured pencil on a flat charcoal field (#23262E), the kind
of original concept plate printed in a tabletop game art book. Three-quarter view
large in the centre, a small front elevation lower left and a small top-down
silhouette lower right, all the same design at the same scale. Not a vector icon,
not clipart, not a 3D render.

SUBJECT: a narrow stone obelisk with four faces, tapering to a blunt sliced top.
A channel is cut down the front face, off centre, and inside it a blade-shaped
sliver floats with a clear gap on every side. Three small shards hang around the
base at different heights, frozen in the air, none of them touching the ground.
The surface is dark polished stone with chipped edges and a visible fracture line
that does not reach the top. No glow, no rays, no energy arcs, no second object.

PALETTE: near-black basalt (#1B1820 to #2E2937), the channel interior in deep
wine (#3A1F35), the floating sliver in hot magenta (#E24BB4) with a pale edge
(#FFC2EA), the shards in the same magenta at a duller value. The charcoal sheet
field stays flat. Every shadow is painted in indigo, not black.

NEGATIVE

SETTINGS
1024 x 1024, square. High detail. The magenta must hold at 96px, so do not let it
become a thin outline.

 4. crate_celestial  -  "Celestial Core"
================================================================================

Intent: a sphere in a cage. The core is the prize and the cage is the promise that
it can be held, and the drawing has to make the cage look older than the sphere.

MODEL NOTES
- A near-perfect sphere, 2.6 studs across, sitting in a three-ring cage.
- The rings are at three different angles, each one a flat band, not a tube.
- Two rings are complete, one is broken at the front with the loose end curled.
- The sphere is opaque, pale, and clearly solid; it is not a light source.
- The cage sits on a low plinth with a stepped edge, 0.4 studs tall.
- Celestial uses the gold band, not white; white would read as a UI element.

PROMPT
Hand-painted model sheet for a game object, drawn by a professional illustrator
in gouache, ink and coloured pencil on a flat charcoal field (#23262E), the kind
of original concept plate printed in a tabletop game art book. Three-quarter view
large in the centre, a small front elevation lower left and a small top-down
silhouette lower right, all the same design at the same scale. Not a vector icon,
not clipart, not a 3D render.

SUBJECT: a solid pale sphere held inside a cage of three flat metal rings set at
three different angles. Two rings are complete; the third is broken at the front
and its loose end curls outward. The cage rests on a low stepped plinth. The sphere
is matte and opaque, with a faint marbled vein running across it, and it does not
glow. The rings are dented and one is repaired with a darker splice. No stars, no
orbit lines, no light, no second object.

PALETTE: bone-white sphere (#EDE4D2) with a warm marbled vein (#C9A96B), the cage
in old gold (#B08A3E) with a darker splice (#6E5526), the plinth in grey-brown

 5. crate_singularity  -  "Singularity Vault"
================================================================================

Intent: a door that was built to stay shut. The last crate in the ladder should
look like infrastructure, not loot: heavy, bolted, and slightly too large for the
room.

MODEL NOTES
- A rectangular vault door, 3.6 x 4.2 x 0.9 studs, standing upright on a base.
- A central wheel with four spokes, the only circular element.
- Eight bolt heads around the rim, unevenly spaced by a finger's width.
- A vertical seam down the middle that does not open: the door is one piece.
- Two hinges on the left, each the size of a forearm.
- The door face carries three horizontal ribs, one bent out of line.
- Singularity is the deepest purple in the set, almost black at the edges.

PROMPT
Hand-painted model sheet for a game object, drawn by a professional illustrator
in gouache, ink and coloured pencil on a flat charcoal field (#23262E), the kind
of original concept plate printed in a tabletop game art book. Three-quarter view
large in the centre, a small front elevation lower left and a small top-down
silhouette lower right, all the same design at the same scale. Not a vector icon,
not clipart, not a 3D render.

SUBJECT: a heavy rectangular vault door standing upright on a short welded base,
thicker than a door has any right to be. A four-spoke wheel sits dead centre. Eight
oversized bolt heads ring the face, spaced slightly unevenly, one of them chewed by
a wrench. A vertical seam runs down the middle but does not open: it is one cast
piece. Three horizontal ribs cross the upper half and the lowest is bent out of
line. Two massive hinges hang on the left edge. The face is scratched by something
that was dragged across it. No opening, no light behind it, no second object.

PALETTE: deep violet-black face (#211A2E to #332646), the wheel and bolts in cold
iron (#4C4658) with worn edges (#8B8299), the weld seam in burnt umber (#6B4326).
The charcoal sheet field stays flat. Highlights are narrow and hard, never glossy.

NEGATIVE
glow, bloom, portal, light behind the door, smoke, sparks, sci-fi interface, text,
letters, numbers, logo, watermark, photograph, 3D render, glossy plastic, chrome,
vector, clipart, floor, background scenery, person.

SETTINGS
1024 x 1024, square. High detail. The door must read at 96px as a slab with a
wheel, so keep the silhouette heavy and simple.

 6. x2_star_matter  -  the boost badge (redraw)
================================================================================

Intent: one hour of double Star-Matter should feel like a gift, not a coupon. The
existing badge (AssetManifest.UiArt.x2_star_matter, id 118909357848534) already
sits at the centre of the starter card and the Store shelf, so the redraw keeps its
silhouette: a round gold plate with a bolt cut through it. The art is shown at
96px on the starter card, so it is drawn for that size first.

MODEL NOTES
- Perfect circle, 1:1, transparent corners.
- The bolt is a single cut-out, not an outline over the plate.
- One highlight sweep at upper left, one shadow at lower right.
- No text: the "x2" is a UI label under the plate, never baked into the art.
- The plate must still read as gold at 48px.

PROMPT
Hand-painted game badge illustration, perfectly circular, centred on a square
canvas with fully transparent corners. Painted by a professional illustrator in
gouache, ink and coloured pencil, the kind of original art printed on a tabletop
game box. Rich, saturated, hand-mixed colour. Not a vector icon, not clipart, not
a 3D render, not an app icon.

SUBJECT: a thick gold medallion with a raised rim and a single lightning bolt cut
clean through the face. The bolt is a hole, so the background shows through it.
The rim has three small notches, one at the top, two at the lower sides. The face
is slightly domed and one edge of the bolt is chipped. Nothing else in the frame:
no background, no ground, no second object.

PALETTE: warm gold face (#F2C14E) deepening to amber at the rim (#B9791F), the cut
edge of the bolt in pale cream (#FFF0C4), the inner shadow in burnt umber
(#7A4A17). The ink contour is the only true black and it is painted over once.

NEGATIVE
text, letters, numbers, "x2", star, sparkle, glow, bloom, lens flare, gradient
mesh, vector, flat design, clipart, 3D render, glossy plastic, chrome, background,
ground, second object, watermark, signature.

SETTINGS
1024 x 1024, square, transparent background. High detail at the rim, flat centre.
